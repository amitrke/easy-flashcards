Delete your account and data

Easy Flashcards & Quiz works without an account. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you can delete your account and the data we hold for it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and tap the account icon at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and the cloud copy of your study sets (titles, descriptions, terms and definitions). The sets stored on your device are not removed; uninstalling the app removes them.

If you can no longer open the app

Open an issue at github.com/amitrke/easy-flashcards/issues asking for your account to be deleted. Do not post personal details in the issue; we will tell you what we need to verify it is your account.
