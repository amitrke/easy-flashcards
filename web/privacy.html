Privacy Policy

Easy Flashcards & Quiz (the app). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

The short version

The app works without an account. Your study sets stay on your device unless you choose to sign in. We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking, and never sell or share your data.

Without an account

Study sets you create are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with Google or Apple backs up your study sets and syncs them across your devices. When you sign in we process:

  • Account details from Google or Apple: a user ID, and your name and email address if the provider shares them (Apple may give a private relay address).
  • Your study sets: titles, descriptions, terms and definitions you write.

We use this only to sign you in and to store and sync your sets. It is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data on our behalf and encrypts it in transit and at rest. Only your signed-in account can read or change your sets.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, other than the Google Firebase service that hosts it and the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Apple).

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in the app to stop syncing. Your sets remain on your device.
  • Delete account (Account screen in the app) permanently deletes your account and your cloud copy of your sets.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over, and signing in is meant only for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents and guardians can use the app with their own account to create study sets for their children, and children can use the app without any account. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, open an issue (see below) and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear on this page. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/amitrke/easy-flashcards/issues.
